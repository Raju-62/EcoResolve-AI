# EcoResolve AI — Project Description

## Title
EcoResolve AI — AI-Powered Sustainability Complaint Analyzer

## Primary SDG
SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Secondary relevance: SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy), SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).

## Problem statement
How might we use AI to analyze sustainability-related complaints from a campus or community so that recurring environmental and resource issues can be identified and addressed more efficiently?

## Target users
- Students/staff who report sustainability issues.
- Administrators who review, prioritize, assign and resolve issues.

## AI solution
EcoResolve accepts a natural-language complaint and location, then produces a structured category, subcategory, priority recommendation, concise summary and suggested action. Aggregated complaint data can be analyzed for recurring patterns and sustainability insights.

## Why AI
AI is useful for converting unstructured complaint text into structured information, summarizing reports, detecting patterns across many reports, and supporting administrative decisions at scale.

## Expected impact
- Faster triage of sustainability complaints.
- Better visibility into recurring issues.
- Data-supported maintenance and sustainability interventions.
- A transparent record of AI recommendations and human decisions.

## Prototype
The repository contains a working web prototype with student and administrator flows, backend APIs, database models, AI-analysis adapter, analytics and insight generation.
